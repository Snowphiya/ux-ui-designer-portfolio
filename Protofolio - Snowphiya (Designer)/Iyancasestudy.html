01 / OVERVIEW

Project overview

Iyan Jewels is a jewellery e-commerce design project focused on creating an elegant and user-friendly online shopping experience. The interface explores how users can discover jewellery collections, view product details and move through the shopping journey with ease.

My Role

UX/UI Designer — concept development, user flow, interface design and prototyping.

Tools & Responsibilities

Figma, wireframing, visual design, e-commerce interface design and prototyping.

02 / DESIGN CHALLENGE

Making jewellery shopping effortless

Online jewellery shopping should feel clear, trustworthy and enjoyable. Users need to explore collections, understand product details and make purchase decisions without unnecessary confusion.

This design concept focuses on a clean visual hierarchy, organised product presentation and a straightforward shopping journey.
